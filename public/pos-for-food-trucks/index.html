Frequently Asked Questions

What comes inside the ₹4,999 Food Truck Starter Kit?

The Starter Kit includes the portable Hoin 58mm Bluetooth battery thermal printer, charging cable, starter thermal paper roll, 1-year CafeQR POS software license, and free menu setup!

Can multiple staff punch orders on separate phones simultaneously?

Yes. You can have one person outside taking orders in the queue while another staff member prints kitchen tokens inside the truck.

How does CafeQR handle offline orders?

Every transaction, item quantity, and token number is stored safely in local device memory. Even if your 4G/5G data is disconnected, orders continue printing without pause. Once internet returns, data syncs silently with cloud reports.
