Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use CafeQR for bakeries with custom cake weight pricing?

Yes! CafeQR supports advance cake orders with design notes, pickup dates, advance token payments, and weight-based pricing (0.5 kg, 1 kg, 2 kg).

Does CafeQR connect to weighing scales?

Yes, CafeQR supports barcode scales and loose weight pricing for bakeries, sweet shops, and grocery counters.
